**Cyber Jolen: The Momentum Arena**

**PROJECT OVERVIEW**
Cyber Jolen: The Momentum Arena is a 2D physics game where players use a striker marble to hit and push enemy marbles out of a circular arena. The simulation applies real physics concepts such as impulse, momentum, collisions, and friction to show how force and speed affect movement. It combines simple gameplay with a clear demonstration of basic motion and collision principles.

**PROJECT OBJECTIVES**
Demonstrate how impulse affects a marble’s speed when force is applied over time.
Show conservation of momentum during marble collisions.
Visualize the effect of mass and friction on motion.
Provide an interactive and fun way to learn basic physics concepts.
