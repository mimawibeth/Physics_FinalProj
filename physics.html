Understanding Impulse & Momentum

Impulse (J): Force × Time

When you drag and release, you apply an impulse to the striker. The longer/farther you drag, the greater the impulse!

Momentum (p): Mass × Velocity

Impulse changes momentum! J = Δp. Watch how the striker's momentum changes when you launch it.

Conservation of Momentum:

When marbles collide, total momentum is conserved. See it in action when your striker hits enemies!
